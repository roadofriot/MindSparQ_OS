# Decisions & Open Questions
Resolve these during discovery; assumptions should not be silently hard-coded.

## Organization
- Confirm legal company name and product branding (MindSparQ spelling/format).
- Single company or future multi-branch support?
- What are the operating locations and stock locations?

## Schools and programs
- School status pipeline and required contact fields.
- Class/section naming conventions.
- Course levels and curriculum.
- Program schedule recurrence rules and holidays.
- Teacher assignment conflicts and substitute workflow.
- Student data collection and retention needs.

## Agreements and finance
- Agreement models and pricing examples.
- Fixed vs per-student enrollment calculation rules.
- What is billable and when?
- Tax/invoice requirements; consult accountant.
- Collection workflow and approval thresholds.
- Expense approval thresholds.
- Account opening balances and reconciliation procedure.
- Cash/bank/wallet account list.
- Accounting basis and inventory costing policy.

## Inventory
- Units and SKU conventions.
- Costing method (FIFO/weighted average or another policy) and accounting advice.
- Stock locations and transfer workflow.
- Distribution ownership (school stock vs student assignment vs sale).
- Returns, loss and damage approval.

## HR and attendance
- Office hours and grace periods.
- Geofence radii per site.
- Attendance window, check-in/out and exceptions.
- Location retention and consent.
- Teacher compensation rules and payroll scope.

## Technology
- Maps provider and costs.
- Push/email/SMS providers.
- Offline requirements.
- Supported desktop/mobile versions.
- Hosting, backup, restore targets and budget.
- Whether BS date conversion is display-only or business-entry requirement.

## Governance
- Admin permission delegations.
- Data retention/deletion policy.
- UAT sign-off owners.
- Incident-response owner.
